## C. Why not one master regex
A single combined regex for all themes is ~12,400 characters. **GSC rejects anything over 4,096**,
so a single master pattern cannot be saved — that is why this is split. The packs above are
stem-based rather than phrase-lists, which is how 2,149 keywords fit into 4 filters instead of 17.

## D. Maintenance
- Re-validate after adding keywords: `python3 /app/memory/seo/gsc_regex_packs.py`
  (prints char counts, RE2 safety, and any keyword the packs miss).
- Keep `(?i)` at the front; GSC is case-insensitive by default but Looker Studio is not.
- RE2 has no lookaheads/backreferences — none are used here, so these also work in
  Looker Studio, BigQuery bulk export and Bing's API.
